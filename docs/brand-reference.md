### Typography
- Montserrat — primary / display. Weights 600 / 700 / 800 only. "Closest widely-available companion to the logo's geometric capitals."
- Source Sans 3 — secondary / text. Body at 16px for screen, generous leading; Light 300 only for large intros; Semibold 600 for labels and subheads.
- Cormorant Garamond italic — accent / scripture and pull quotes only. Never for headings, labels or body.
- Web stack: `Montserrat, "Source Sans 3", system-ui`. Never substitute Inter, Arial or Calibri for Montserrat in headings.
- System fallbacks: Arial Black, Arial, Georgia.

Type scale:
| Style | Spec |
|---|---|
| Display | Montserrat 800, 40-56pt, tracking -2% |
| Heading | Montserrat 700, 24-32pt, tracking -1% |
| Subheading | Montserrat 600, 14-18pt, sentence case |
| Eyebrow | Montserrat 700, 8-9pt, caps, tracking +28% |
| Body | Source Sans 3 400, 10-11pt print / 16px web, leading 1.65 |
| Caption | Source Sans 3 400, 8-9pt, 70% navy |
| Scripture | Cormorant Garamond italic, 16-24pt |

## B. "Gracepoint" system (Feb 2026) — parent + three sub-brands

### Brand Identity Guide - RCCG Christ Church (Gracepoint)

Departmental harmony rule: all sub-entity designs (LCC, DC, Genesis) must include the "A Ministry of RCCG Christ Church" tagline or the parent seal in the bottom right corner.

- Gracepoint: The Parent, The Pillar (Navy/Serif)
- Lighthouse: The Bridge (Gold/Minimalist)
- DC: The Focus (Black/Cyan/Tech)
- Genesis: The Spark (Rainbow/Rounded)

Values: Excellence (polished, intentional design), Unity (one body under RCCG Christ Church), Clarity (communicate the Gospel simply, remove visual clutter), Warmth (every design should feel like an open door).

Primary palette (must appear in all church-wide communications):
- RCCG Navy Blue #1A1A5E (Stability & Faith)
- Foundation White #FFFFFF (Purity & Light)
- Gracepoint Gold #D4AF37 (The Lighthouse Accent)

Secondary / departmental accents:
- Lighthouse (Young Adults): Black #1A1A1B and Gold #D4AF37
- Discovery Centre (Teens): Slate Gray #636E72, Cyan #00CEC9 and Gold #D4AF37
- Genesis (Children): Genesis Red #E72F24, Orange #F39221, Blue #21A9E1 and Gold #D4AF37

Typography:
- Headings: Playfair Display (RCCG Christ Church and Lighthouse headers)
- Sub-headings & youth media: Montserrat Bold (DC and Genesis)
- Body: Open Sans, min 16px web, line spacing 1.5

Visual elements:
- Buttons: rectangular, 4px radius (standard) or pill (Genesis). Primary buttons always Gracepoint Gold with white or navy text.
- Icons: linear/outline only, consistent 2pt stroke, same icon set across all departments.
- Imagery: "Candid over staged", real people, natural bright lighting. Text over images gets a 20% navy tint overlay.
- Accessibility: 4.5:1 contrast minimum. Never stretch logos; clear space = 20% of logo width on all sides.

Logo files (`Gracepoint/`): Gracepoint Logo.png, Gracepoint Logo - White Text.png, Gracepoint Logo variant 2.png, Gracepoint Logo variant 2 - White Text.png

### Sub-Brand Guide - Lighthouse Christ Church (LCC) — young adults
Concept: Guidance and Warmth. Classic lighthouse silhouette with a modern circular "Golden Ring".
Tone: Aspirational (future leaders and professionals), Minimalist (white space), Warm.

| Colour | HEX | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Gracepoint Gold | #D4AF37 | The "Ring" in the logo, icons, primary accent lines |
| Lighthouse Black | #1A1A1B | Primary text, elegant dark-mode backgrounds |
| Champagne Cream | #F9F7F2 | Softer alternative to pure white for backgrounds and cards |
| RCCG Navy | #1A1A5E | Formal footers and administrative documents |

Type: Playfair Display (Regular/Bold) headers; Montserrat Light (spaced out) secondary; Open Sans body, light touch.
Assets: subtle gold-to-yellow gradients (lighthouse "glow"); lifestyle photography, bright warm tones, shallow depth of field.
Harmony: at general church events use RCCG Navy background with Gracepoint Gold typography. Tagline: "The Young Adult Expression of RCCG Christ Church, Edmonton."
Logo files (`Lighthouse/`): LCC LOGO - FOR DARK BGS.png, LCC LOGO - FOR LIGHT BGS.png, Lighthouse Logo.png, Lighthouse Logo - White Text.png, Lighthouse Logo - Variant 2 - Dark Text.png, Lighthouse Logo - Variant 2 - White Text.png, Lighthouse Logo Icon Only.png, Lighthouse Logo - White Icon Only.png

### Sub-Brand Guide - Discovery Centre (DC) — teens, grades 7-12
Concept: Focus and Purpose. "Compass" logo, a teenager finding identity and purpose in Christ. High-contrast, technical, bold.
Tone: Direct (no fluff), Modern (tech, streetwear, digital culture), Purposeful.

| Colour | HEX | Usage |
|---|---|---|
| Obsidian Black | #1A1A1B | Backgrounds, heavy headers, streetwear-style apparel |
| Foundation White | #FFFFFF | Primary logo colour and body text on dark |
| Focus Cyan | #00CEC9 | "Target" highlights, links, high-energy accents |
| Action Slate | #636E72 | Secondary text, borders, subtle textures |

Type: Montserrat Extra Bold (all caps) primary; Montserrat Medium accent; Montserrat Regular body.
Assets: subtle graph-paper / grid overlays; "crosshair" motif (broken circles from the logo) as photo frames; duotone photos (navy-to-cyan or black-to-white).
Harmony: every DC piece carries "A Youth Expression of RCCG Christ Church, Edmonton" in the footer; a single thin Gracepoint Gold line at the very bottom as the parent "signature".
Logo files (`DC/`): DC Logo.png, DC Logo - White.png, DC Logo - icon only.png, DC Logo - icon only white.png

### Sub-Brand Guide - Genesis Christ Church — children
Concept: Joy and Revelation. Playful "bubble" typeface with radiant rays of light. Safe, fun, accessible.
Tone: Playful, Simple (big icons, short sentences, friendly faces), Radiant ("sunny" and optimistic).

| Colour | HEX | Role |
|---|---|---|
| Genesis Red | #E72F24 | Primary action (Stop, Look, Listen) |
| Genesis Orange | #F39221 | Energy & creativity |
| Genesis Blue | #21A9E1 | Trust & learning |
| Genesis Purple | #6E3996 | Wisdom & royalty (The King's Kids) |
| RCCG Navy | #1A1A5E | The anchor: footer borders only |
| Gracepoint Gold | #D4AF37 | Light, consistency across all expressions |

Type: Fredoka One primary header; Montserrat Medium secondary; Open Sans body.
Assets: rounded corners everywhere (radius >= 20px); the "ray" motif as pointers to important info (e.g. a "Register Here" button); dot / wave / star patterns at 10% opacity.
Harmony: parent RCCG Christ Church logo always on Genesis pages; white base, keep it bright (no dark backgrounds); tagline "The Children's Ministry of RCCG Christ Church, Edmonton."
Logo files (`Genesis/`): Genesis Logo.png, Genesis Logo - black.png, Genesis Logo - white.png
