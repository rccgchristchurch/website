About RCCG

The Redeemed Christian Church of God

The Redeemed Christian Church of God (RCCG) has the vision to spread the Word of God to the ends of the earth. It is a Bible believing church. The General Overseer of the church is Pastor E.A. Adeboye. Christ Church Parish, Edmonton is one of over 5,000 RCCG parishes around the world. Final

Mission
  1. To make heaven.
  2. To take as many people with us.
  3. To have a member of RCCG in every family of all nations.
  4. To accomplish No. 1 above, holiness will be our lifestyle.
  5. To accomplish No. 2 and 3 above, we will plant churches within five minutes walking distance in every city and town of developing countries and within five minutes driving distance in every city and town of developed countries.

For parents

Check-in and safety

Check your child in at the Genesis desk before the service and keep the matching pick-up tag; only the person holding that tag can collect your child. Tell us about allergies or medical needs at check-in and we will note them for the teachers. Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026 Placeholder · draft policy, needs leadership review
ON THIS PAGEWhat we collectHow we use itForms and prayer requestsCookiesContact

What we collect

We collect only what you choose to give us. When you fill in a form on this site, that is your name, contact details and whatever you write in the message. When you give online, the payment provider handles your card details; we never see or store them. The site itself does not create accounts, build profiles or track you across other websites. Placeholder · review with leadership before launch

How we use it

We use your details to do what you asked: reply to your message, arrange a visit, add you to a house fellowship, process a dedication or thanksgiving, or send a tax receipt. We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone outside the church, except the service providers we rely on to run the website, process gifts and send email, and only for that purpose.

“Let all that you do be done in love.” 1 Corinthians 16:14

Forms and prayer requests

Form submissions go to the church office mailbox and the pastor or team leader responsible for that form. Prayer requests are read only by the pastors and the prayer team, are treated as confidential, and are not shared in services without your consent. We keep form submissions for as long as needed to follow up, and no longer than two years, after which they are deleted. You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete what we hold about you by emailing info@rccgchristchurch.ca.
