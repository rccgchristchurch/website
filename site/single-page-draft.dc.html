What to expect

Three simple steps

01

Arrive and park

Free parking on site. Doors open 30 minutes before the service and a greeter will point you to seating and the children's check-in.

02

Worship with us

Lively praise, prayer and a Bible-based message. Services run about two hours and are also streamed online.

03

Connect after service

Stay for refreshments, meet a pastor at the welcome desk, and fill in a first-timer card so we can follow up.
